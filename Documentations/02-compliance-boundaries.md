# Compliance Boundaries

## Allowed Direction

The platform may support:

- Official API integrations
- Authorized customer data imports
- Public data collection where permitted by source terms
- Consent-based messaging workflows
- Review invitation flows for real customers
- AI-assisted content drafting with human review
- Brand monitoring and response recommendations
- Audit logs, permissions, and data governance

## Disallowed Direction

The platform must not be designed for:

- Fake reviews or fabricated testimonials
- Posting reviews on behalf of consumers
- Unauthorized scraping or automated data collection
- Account purchasing, account farming, or account matrix operations
- Evading platform risk controls or detection systems
- Bypassing CAPTCHA, OTP, liveness, or identity verification
- Unsolicited bulk messaging or spam workflows
- Collecting or using personal data without a lawful basis

## Design Standard

When a feature touches user data, platform data, outbound messaging, reviews, or generated marketing claims, the design must include:

- Data source and authorization basis
- Consent and opt-out handling where applicable
- Human review or approval step where risk is material
- Audit trail for generated or published content
- Retention and deletion policy
